Knowledge Management System (KMS)

Project Overview

A centralized platform developed to simplify creating, storing, organizing, managing, and sharing information. It enables users to access relevant knowledge, retrieve important resources, and maintain information systematically, improving accessibility, collaboration and productivity.

Problem Being Solved

Traditional methods make it difficult to store, organize, search, and retrieve knowledge efficiently. This project provides a centralized platform to securely manage, access, and share information, reducing duplication and improving accessibility.

Key Contributions

  • Centralized Knowledge Repository: unified storage and systematic management of structured information.
  • Resource Lifecycle: creation, uploading, categorization, and editing of knowledge resources.
  • Fast Resource Retrieval: quick search and retrieval of stored resources.
  • User-Friendly Interface: accessible, responsive design across devices.
  • Efficient Collaboration: reduces redundancy and boosts productivity.
